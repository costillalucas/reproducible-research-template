On Monday the 28th the lab took the same field in all three colours, with the focus fixed on green and no-sample photos. It is the first multispectral test with real data, which is what the project title promised. The raw photos of the three colours agree well with each other, between 0.71 and 0.78: it is the same field. But the reconstructions only agree in what the objective already sees, up to 0.52, and in the fine detail almost not at all, 0.06 at most. If that detail belonged to the sample, it would have to appear in all three colours. Also, only green, the colour in focus, looks like the sample; in red and blue the particles come out enlarged and with a grid texture, and refocusing them numerically does not fix it. In other words: the real multispectral combination cannot be done yet, because first each colour has to give a reliable reconstruction. Capture detail: that day's dark frames came out saturated, so we used Friday's, which are from the same camera.
