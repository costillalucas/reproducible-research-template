The agents worked out that the July capture had very little light and proposed repeating it with several exposure times. The lab did it on 24 Sep: 4,563 images. The centre of the map is direct light; the edges are large angles, with little signal but carrying the fine-detail information. It also served to measure the geometry: the matrix is about 80 mm away. The 131 LEDs come from the structural signal over the noise of each photo (results/captura_2026-09-24/stats_por_led/an.py). The July figure of 12 is a different measure: LEDs with at least 10 % of the brightest one's brightness, out of 182; it is not a direct comparison, but it shows that in July there was almost no light outside the centre.
