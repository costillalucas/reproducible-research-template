Why those two LEDs

Where the pupil edge falls depends on centring and on NA·z

The objective's acceptance cone projected onto the array is a circle of radius z·tan(θ_NA), about 7 mm. Inside, bright field; outside, dark field. An LED right on the edge is neither one nor the other, and the model with a binary pupil cannot represent it: hence the streaks. Two causes move the edge: the axis shift (panel B; in red the die is shifted +1.06 mm along the rows, plus the off-centring of the capture) and the product NA·z (panel C). Consequences: a centring protocol before measuring, and an edge calibration, which measures NA·z and not z alone. Schematic; the 2.5 mm shift in panel B is illustrative.
