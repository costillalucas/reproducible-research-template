Group talk · 17 November 2026

Fourier Ptychography

Mini-course and project status

Lucas Costilla
LEC · IFIBA/CONICET

40-minute talk in two parts. First a mini-course of about 25 minutes: basics, oblique illumination and Fourier, reconstruction and validation, with three audience questions. Then about 13 minutes of results from our setup and 2 minutes of closing. Approximate timing: cover and motivation 2 min; block 1 basics 6 min; block 2 illumination 5 min; block 3 reconstruction 3 min; block 4 validation 4 min; results 13 min; closing 2 min. The questions have a hidden answer: it is revealed with a click after letting the audience think for 30–60 seconds. At the end there is an appendix (after the closing) with the geometry-to-Fourier calculations and real FFT data, in case someone asks.
