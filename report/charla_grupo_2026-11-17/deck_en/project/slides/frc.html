FRC between two independent reconstructions

The LEDs are split by parity (row+col), like a checkerboard. Each half is reconstructed separately. In each frequency ring we measure how well the two transforms agree: the noise and artefacts specific to each half do not agree, whereas the reproducible signal does. C2 requires agreement (FRC ≥ 1/7) outside the objective's band. The 1/7 threshold is a cryo-EM convention (Rosenthal and Henderson 2003), also used in localization microscopy (Nieuwenhuizen 2013). What really supports the criterion is the comparison with the null control, which shuffles the dark field. The curve is schematic and does not show measured data.
