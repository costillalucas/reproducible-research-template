Bright field and dark field

Sketch with the October geometry (NA 0.067, z 102 mm). With the current measurement (NA 0.072, z 99.5 mm) the edge moves to ±7.2 mm and the split is the same: neighbors (6 mm) in bright field, diagonals (8.5 mm) in dark field.

Only the LEDs whose angle falls inside the acceptance cone of the objective give bright field: the direct light enters the objective. The others give dark field: only the light that the sample diffracts toward larger angles enters, and that is the high-frequency information FPM uses to surpass the resolution of the objective. Calculation: z·tan(asin NA) = edge of the cone; with NA 0.072 and z 99.5 mm it gives about 7.2 mm. Neighbor at 6 mm: bright field. Diagonal at 8.49 mm (NA_illum ≈ 0.085): dark field. This connects with the null control: mixing in the dark field destroys precisely the high-frequency information. The drawing has the vertical scale compressed.
