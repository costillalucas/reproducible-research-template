Appendix · extra question

We turn on an LED 6 mm from the axis, with the array 100 mm from the sample. What shifts in Fourier space, and by how much? Is the photo bright field or dark field?

The spectrum shifts

k = sin θ / λ ≈ 0.095 µm⁻¹

Bright field

0.095 < NA/λ = 0.111: the zero order enters

FPM mini-course · answer on one click

(1.5 min) This is the heart of FPM: tilting the illumination is equivalent to shifting the object spectrum relative to the fixed pupil (or the pupil over the spectrum). The comparison is between sin θ and NA: if sin θ < NA, the zero order enters and the photo is bright field; otherwise only diffracted light enters and it is dark field. With our geometry, the 4 neighbours on the cross (6 mm) are bright field and the diagonals (8.5 mm, sin θ ≈ 0.085) are dark field. (In the talk, the bright field/dark field slide is in block 2.)
