What we ruled out

Ruling out causes, one by one

(1.5 min) The central question of these months: why does the model predict so much worse with real data than in simulation (residual ~0.68 vs 0.15)? We have been ruling out causes with evidence. Noise: 4× more exposure lowered the error by only 9 %, and 2× (13/11) left C1 unchanged. Iterations: from 20 to 400 epochs C1 goes from 0.597 to 0.585 (C2 does keep rising: 0.185 at 160 epochs, 0.210 at 400; the null control at 400 is missing). Array position: ±1° rotation and 1–3° tilt are not visible in C1. Aberrations: recovering the pupil (EPRY) never improved real data. Magnification: the micrometer gave 1.290 µm/px and excluded the nominal 2.28×. Sphere thickness: the BPM simulation changes things by 1–4 %. Also ruled out, among others: numerically correcting the intensity of each photo (residual 0.71 → 0.67), λ as the cause of the +10 % in NA, more LEDs (31×31), crop size, soft pupil edge, focus and axis for the NA·z jump, meniscus and DMSO layer, joint 3-colour reconstruction, preprocessing artefacts in C2 (null control 0.008). What remains: a non-geometric model error, with candidates stray light (~30 % in the flat), brightness of each LED and pupil shape.
