Geometry · 01–20/10

Two LEDs at the pupil edge produced streaks in the phase

With LEDs (18,14) and (18,16): streaks 0.52 rad

Without those two LEDs: streaks 0.01 rad

Demodulated red phase, same field, epoch 40, scale ±2 rad. Separately, the micrometer gave 1.290 µm/px (M 2.48) and ruled out the nominal 2.28×.

The streaks had a period of ~68 µm. Those two diagonal LEDs of the first ring cross the pupil edge and were being normalized as bright field. When they are excluded, the streaks drop from ~0.5 to 0.01 rad. The same result is obtained with the nominal geometry without those LEDs (0.02 rad), so it does not depend on fine-tuning the geometry. We also measured the array offsets per colour (R +1.06, G +0.63, B +0.15 mm) and ruled out a geometric origin for the phase-volume deficit in green and blue. This is the same type of problem that reappeared on 11/11 with the rounding in k: an LED classified as bright field when the model treats it differently.
