Audience question · 1 of 3

Our objective has NA 0.07 and we use red light (λ = 0.63 µm). With coherent illumination, what is the finest period that reaches the camera? What NA would be needed to see a period of 1.4 µm?

Minimum period

p = λ / NA ≈ 9 µm

For 1.4 µm

NA = λ / p ≈ 0.45: the FPM goal

FPM Mini-course · answer in two clicks: first the pupil (light blue), then the FPM goal (orange)

(1.5 min) Leave 30–60 s for them to think. It connects with the previous slide: the coherent cutoff is NA/λ, so the minimum period is λ/NA. First click: 9 µm. Second click: for 1.4 µm an NA ≈ 0.45 is needed, about 6 times that of the objective. If someone asks why 9 µm and not 4.5: 4.5 µm is the incoherent limit, λ/(2NA). In FPM each picture is coherent (an almost point-like LED), and the synthetic aperture is built by adding LEDs.
