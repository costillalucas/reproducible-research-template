FPM Mini-course · in pictures · simulation (grid 128, pupil 18, pitch 10)

Both panels are simulations of the model in the interactive Fourier page v2: thin sample, one coherent LED at a time, no noise. On the left, fringes whose period is finer than what the pupil lets through: with the central LED the side peaks fall outside and the picture comes out flat. With the oblique LED, the window shifts and the central term and one of the peaks enter: fringes appear without enlarging the aperture. On the right, the full scan: each LED opens a different window of the spectrum, and the union of all of them is the synthetic aperture. The dark-field pictures are shown with their own enhancement because they are much dimmer.
