Solver · 11/11

The solver rounds k and can flip an LED from bright field to dark field

With crops of 200–400 px it is safe. With 20 px, the LED changes class in 20 of 25 crops.

500 nm particles: a dark-field LED becomes bright field in 20 of 25 crops of 20 px. 5 µm spheres: the LED (17,16), at 95.5 % of the radius, becomes dark field in all 18 spheres. Rounding error: median 15 %, maximum 27 % of the LED step at 20 px; 13 % at 40 px; 2.6 % at 200 px. The driver classifies with the exact k (reconstruir_plan.py) and the model uses the rounded k (spectral_ops.py). The null control erases the particle: the structure comes from the dark field. The FWHM change from 5.3 to 3.1 µm is NOT a resolution factor: the fit measures the lobe in the raw image and the core in the reconstruction, and 3 µm is still far above 0.5 µm. The LED next to the axis is physically close to the pupil edge, so any geometry change of a few percent can trigger this problem again. The fix in the solver has not been implemented yet.
