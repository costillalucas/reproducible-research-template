(1 min) On to the data. Everything that follows uses this setup: a 2.5× objective with NA 0.07 (limit of about 5.5 µm), a 21 × 21 array of red, green and blue LEDs with 6 mm pitch at about 100 mm from the sample, a Mono12 camera with 1.29 µm per pixel at the sample measured with a micrometer, and 5 µm spheres in DMSO as the main sample. The criteria were written down before looking at the real data, so as not to tune the threshold to the result. C1 is the prediction of held-out LEDs (lower is better); C2 is the FRC between halves (checkerboard), averaged over the band that the objective alone does not see, with threshold 1/7; the null control shuffles the dark field. The current measured geometry is z 99.5 mm and NA ≈ 0.070–0.072.
