Red capture of 03/11, rows 360:760, columns 80:480, 160 epochs; 516 µm field. The yellow box marks a 100 µm region used for a zoom, not shown. The null with the shuffled dark field gives C2 0.008, so it is not a preprocessing artefact. C2 keeps rising with the epochs (0.185 at 160, 0.210 at 400) without plateauing. The measured physical geometry (z 99.5 mm, NA 0.070, λ 0.617 µm) improves C1 relative to the previous one, but enlarging only the pupil improves it even more: what matters in the reconstruction is NA·z. About the FRC: the two halves of the checkerboard are reconstructed separately and compared ring by ring in Fourier; the correlation drops below 1/7 at k = 0.426 µm⁻¹, a period of 2.35 µm (October estimator: Tukey 0.2, last k above threshold; on 28/09 the same estimator gave 3.55 µm). It measures what the two halves reproduce in common, NOT the resolution against a known object: do not convert it into an 'effective NA'. The 500 nm sample (next slide) gives objects 3.0–3.2 µm wide in each half, but they are aggregates, not a clean PSF; the FRC can be optimistic because both halves share the sample, the preprocessing, the model and its errors.
